How these figures are counted
  • Attendance is counted only when the booking is marked confirmed through the attendance process. A past date, ticket confirmation or reservation alone does not establish attendance.
  • Visits count the guests whose attendance was agreed, including companions. A reservation for three people with two confirmed attendees counts as two visits. Hosts are excluded.
  • Member guests counts distinct member accounts that booked and personally attended. Companion names are shown for attendance records, but are not assumed to identify unique people across bookings.
  • A completed reciprocal exchange means the first attendance and all agreed return places are confirmed. A group swap counts as one exchange. A waived or closed promise is not a completed exchange.
  • Past reservations awaiting confirmation use the dinner's end time, or its start time if no end was supplied.
  • Offered places are the places published on the platform. They do not measure spare college tickets or guests arranged outside it.
  • Figures reflect the current booking and exchange records. Confirmed attendance is retained even if an event is subsequently marked cancelled.
