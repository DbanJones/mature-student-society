Whatever your subject

  • The Faculty administrator is the operational expert: timetables, forms, submission rules, the current written rule.
  • The course organiser is for anything affecting a whole paper, lab or placement group.
  • The subject librarian will save you hours — ask for a five-minute route through the catalogue.
  • Ask early: “Is this a University rule, a Faculty rule, or my college's practice?” The answer decides who can fix it.
