Wellbeing

You're not alone — and we're here to help.

At MSS, we understand that mature students at Cambridge face unique challenges — often juggling academic pressures with careers, family responsibilities, or major life changes. Many are new to the area, to the university system, or even to the UK itself. It's easy to feel isolated or overwhelmed.

We offer

☕ A Mature Students Talking Group

A weekly in-person support group run with the University's Student Services — a confidential space to talk about academic pressure, isolation, or adjusting to a new environment.

🧭 Practical advice

Housing, healthcare, childcare, course deadlines — peer-to-peer guidance and “hive mind” solutions from people who've been there.

📣 Advocacy

Help finding the right channels for department or college issues — or speaking up on your behalf.

🤝 Qualified support

Some wellbeing team members have professional training in counselling, coaching, or mental health first aid. Not a replacement for professional services, but an informed, compassionate bridge to further help.
