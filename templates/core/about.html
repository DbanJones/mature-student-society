Who we're for

  • Those returning to academia after a career or caring responsibilities.
  • Finally pursuing long-held academic ambitions or career-change qualifications.
  • Or just that little bit older than most of your peers (or just feel older!).

Our community includes

  • Undergraduate and postgraduate students (MPhils, PhDs)
  • Postdocs, research fellows, and university researchers
  • Professional and Continuing Education students
  • Faculty and lab staff, librarians, and other university employees
  • Visiting scholars and researchers
  • The partners and children of any of the above
