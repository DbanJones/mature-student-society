One night, properly done

Once a year the society swaps campus cafés and pub snugs for candlelight, white tablecloths and a string quartet. The Winter Ball is our flagship evening: a welcome drink, a proper three-course dinner, dancing until the small hours — and the rare pleasure of seeing your seminar neighbours in black tie.

It is, emphatically, not just for students: partners are warmly invited, and the timing (school-night-proof, carriages at one) is chosen with families in mind.

The evening

  1. 19:00Champagne reception — welcome drinks and canapés by the fire
  2. 19:45Dinner is served — three courses, wine on the table, dietary needs catered
  3. 21:30Speeches & toasts — short, we promise
  4. 22:00The band strikes up — dance floor opens, casino tables and quiet lounge alongside
  5. 23:30Midnight fare — bacon rolls and hot chocolate for the survivors
  6. 01:00Carriages — taxis pre-bookable at the cloakroom

Dress code

Black tie & evening wear — or the nearest your wardrobe allows. National dress and vintage are actively encouraged; gowns may be worn. Nobody is turned away over a missing bow tie.

Tickets

RSVP on the portal reserves your place{% if ball and ball.capacity %} (capacity {{ ball.capacity }}){% endif %}; payment details follow by email. Partner tickets bought the same way — just RSVP for two and note it in the form.

Getting home

We finish at 01:00. The cloakroom desk can pre-book taxis from 22:00, and the venue is a ten-minute walk from the city-centre ranks.

Questions, answered

Can I bring my partner?
Yes — partners and guests are a core part of the night, not an afterthought.
I don't dance. Is this still for me?
The quiet lounge and casino tables are open all night, and the dinner alone is worth the ticket.
What about dietary requirements?
The menu covers vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and halal as standard; a form circulates two weeks before the night for anything else.
Is there parking?
Limited blue-badge parking at the venue — email us and we'll reserve a space. Otherwise the Grand Arcade car park is open all night.
I can no longer make it — can I get a refund?
Full refunds up to a fortnight before; after that we'll offer your place to the waiting list first.
