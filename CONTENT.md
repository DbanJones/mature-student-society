## About Us copy

> Founded in September 2024, the Mature Student Society (MSS) has quickly grown to
> nearly 1,000 members. Our mission is to make the full Cambridge experience
> accessible to everyone — regardless of age, background, or life path.
>
> **Who we're for**: those returning to academia after a career or caring
> responsibilities; finally pursuing long-held academic ambitions or career-change
> qualifications; or just that little bit older than most of your peers (or just
> feel older!).
>
> **Our community includes**: undergraduate and postgraduate students (MPhils,
> PhDs); postdocs, research fellows, and university researchers; Professional and
> Continuing Education students; faculty and lab staff, librarians, and other
> university employees; visiting scholars and researchers; the partners and
> children of any of the above.

## What We Do (activity list)

- College lunches & tours — get to know the University's 31 colleges
- Pub nights — explore Cambridge's pub scene
- Formals and formal-swaps — the traditional Cambridge dining experience
- Research "flashtalks" — present your research and listen to others
- 50+ sub-groups — from parenting lawyers to LGBT+
- Networking — events for making useful contacts
- Walks & running — explore the local area, jog together
- Supper Clubs — Cambridge's range of international cheap-eat cuisines
- Wellbeing coffee meets — share concerns and help others
- Outings — museum/gallery trips and getting out of Cambridge
- Advice and advocacy — hive-mind help and official representation

## Wellbeing copy (full page)

> At MSS, we understand that mature students at Cambridge face unique challenges —
> often juggling academic pressures with careers, family responsibilities, or major
> life changes. Many are new to the area, to the university system, or even to the
> UK itself. It's easy to feel isolated or overwhelmed. You're not alone — and
> we're here to help.
>
> **We offer:**
> - A Mature Students Talking Group: weekly in-person support group run with the
>   University's Student Services — a confidential space to talk about academic
>   pressure, isolation, or adjusting to a new environment.
> - Practical advice: housing, healthcare, childcare, course deadlines — peer-to-peer
>   guidance and "hive mind" solutions.
> - Advocacy: help finding the right channels for department or college issues — or
>   speaking up on your behalf.
> - Qualified support: some wellbeing team members have professional training in
>   counselling, coaching, or mental health first aid. Not a replacement for
>   professional services, but an informed, compassionate bridge to further help.
>
> Contact: maturesoc@cambridgesu.co.uk
